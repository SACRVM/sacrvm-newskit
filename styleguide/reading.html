Newskit styleguide · Phase 1 · Type specimen

The reading layer

Appkit's look, specialised for reading: the ~40rem prose measure, the article type scale, and one new face — Oswald, condensed and uppercase — carrying the news signature. Everything else on this page is appkit's own tokens, in light, dark and auto.

This page is the proof for phase 1. The body you are reading is Inter at 17px on --text-read — softened a step off the pure foreground seed so long passages don't halate on the dark ground, while staying comfortably AA in both schemes. Lines cap at the --measure of 40rem, which lands around 68 characters: long enough to carry an argument, short enough that the eye finds the next line without effort. Flip the theme toggle in the corner — every colour on this page re-derives from appkit's seeds, so the whole specimen re-themes at once, including the accent-as-text tiers like this link and the kicker above the headline.

The headline above is the one deliberate addition newskit makes to appkit's voice: Oswald 600, condensed, uppercase, tight line-height. It is the signature of the reference treatment — the news feeling comes from structure and this display face, never from a separate palette or a warm-paper skin.

The section tier

An h2 steps the same display voice down to section scale. In a finished article this heading usually arrives inside a sac-section-head with its accent marker — that block is phase 2; the bare scale must already hold up on its own, because hand-authored articles are plain HTML first.

The entry tier

An h3 is the title weight of a record card — Oswald 500, the lighter of the two weights the kit ships. Only these two weights exist; the face is subset to latin and latin-ext exactly like the kit's Inter and Outfit, self-hosted, no CDN.

The subhead tier

And an h4 marks a run of prose inside a section. Below the headline tiers, ordinary prose furniture keeps its manners:

  • Unordered lists sit inside the measure, markers on --text-muted.
  • Emphasis is italic; importance is a weight and ink step, not a colour.
  • Inline code takes the kit's quiet 6% wash and the small radius.
  1. Ordered lists share the same rhythm.
  2. Nothing on this page falls below the 0.7rem micro-type floor.

A plain blockquote stays quiet — hairline-led, muted ink. The display-weight emphasis line is a different animal (sac-pullquote, phase 2).

What this page deliberately does not show: record cards, source chips, cite markers, the provenance strip, the chapter rail. Those are the core primitives and the trust layer — phase 2 — and they will sit on the tokens proven here: --accent-soft and --warm-soft for the tint planes, --display-font for every headline, --measure for every line of prose.
